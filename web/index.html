🛡️ Stay safe: quick tips on prize-draw scams
  • Never pay a fee, “postage”, “processing” or “tax” to claim a prize you've supposedly won. Genuine free entries never need card details.
  • Check the promoter is named, has Terms & Conditions, a UK address/company number and a free (postal) entry route if tickets are sold.
  • Be wary of urgency (“act now”), surprise “you've been selected” messages, link-shorteners and odd web addresses; enter via the official site – not links in DMs.
  • Set a budget and stick to it (this page shows how a £30/month budget stretches on paid draws). Free help: BeGambleAware.
