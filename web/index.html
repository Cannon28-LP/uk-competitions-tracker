  • Never pay a fee, “postage”, “processing” or “tax” to claim a prize. Genuine free entries never need card details.
  • Check the promoter is named and has Terms & Conditions, a UK address or company number, and a free postal entry route if tickets are sold.
  • Be wary of urgency (“act now”), surprise “you've been selected” messages, link-shorteners and odd web addresses. Enter via the official site, not links in DMs.
